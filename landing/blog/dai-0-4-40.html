Release 12 October 2026 · v0.4.40

Your miner just learned to check real prices.

A smaller release than most, but one you will notice right away: your miner can go check real market prices on its own, SICI joined the menu, and a couple of rough edges got sanded down.

0.4.39 → 0.4.40 Smaller release, bigger toolbox
01
New skill

Your miner can check real prices now — no account needed

Ask your miner's assistant something like "what's this token worth" or "what's going on with this network today," and it used to be limited to what it already knew. Now it can actually go look.

Seventeen new ways to answer a question shipped in 0.4.40 — live prices and market data from CoinGecko, CoinPaprika, DexPaprika and Crypto.com, network information from Avalanche and LayerZero, cross-chain routing from LI.FI, plus ten more covering everyday questions about wallets, vaults, news and docs. There's also a small library of plain-language notes on how lending protocols like Euler actually work, in case you'd rather read than ask.

Every single one of them works with no sign-up, no account and no API key. You don't configure anything — it's just there.

02
Buy DAI

SICI joins the menu, and the price you see is a real one

SICI now has its own spot in the top menu, next to DAI, Framework, Wallet and Explorer.

And the price shown on the Buy DAI page is no longer a placeholder — it's read live from the trading pool itself (currently on the test network while things get wired up) and links straight through to Uniswap when you're ready to buy.

Also in this release

Small, but you'll notice them within a day of running it.

One less field during setup

The desktop app used to ask for a separate Solana address during onboarding that nothing ever actually used. It's gone — setup is a step shorter, and nothing about your mining wallet changes.

Typing an amount feels right

On the Send screen, the number you type now shows up directly in the big display instead of in a separate box underneath it. One field, doing what it looks like it does.

One official home for everything

The install script, the docs links and the update checks all point to the same place now — github.com/iamaihub/dai-miner.

Put an idle computer to work

A Mac mini, an old gaming PC, a server with spare hours. Download it, pick a model, and it starts earning while you get on with your day.
